# The eight claims — claim by claim

Each document states the strongest reasonable version of the claim, gives a
plain-language answer, then the cited evidence, the steelman, and the
bottom line.

1. ["Wood is renewable, so it's carbon neutral"](carbon-neutral.md) —
   more CO₂ than coal up front; decades of carbon debt; black + brown
   carbon on top.
2. ["It's natural — humans have always burned wood"](natural-safe.md) —
   same toxin classes as tobacco smoke; IARC Group 2A.
3. ["Modern eco-stoves are clean"](modern-stoves-clean.md) — ~450× a gas
   boiler's PM2.5 by the government's own emission factors.
4. ["Traffic and industry are far bigger problems"](traffic-worse.md) —
   largest UK/EU source of primary PM2.5; the only one still rising.
5. ["It's my home — my stove only affects me"](only-affects-me.md) —
   secondhand smoke at street scale; ~100× hotspots through closed windows.
6. ["It's within legal limits, so it's safe"](within-limits-safe.md) —
   no known safe level of PM2.5 (WHO); legal ≠ safe.
7. ["Banning wood burning hurts people who can't afford alternatives"](fuel-poverty.md) —
   ~92% of UK burners have another way to heat.
8. ["I burn dry, seasoned wood correctly — so mine is fine"](dry-wood-proper-use.md) —
   best practice lowers a terrible baseline; it doesn't fix it.
