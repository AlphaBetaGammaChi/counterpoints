## Official reports & statistics

- **Chief Medical Officer's Annual Report 2022: Air Pollution** (UK) —
  air pollution the largest environmental threat to health; emission
  factors for domestic heating (Fig. 10).
  [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/chief-medical-officers-annual-report-2022-air-pollution)
- **Defra / NAEI — Emissions of air pollutants in the UK (PM10 & PM2.5)** —
  domestic combustion the largest/second-largest source of primary
  PM2.5; the only rising source.
  [gov.uk statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/emissions-of-air-pollutants/emissions-of-air-pollutants-in-the-uk-particulate-matter-pm10-and-pm25)
- **Environmental Audit Committee written evidence (AIR0018)** — 2022
  source shares: domestic burning 29%, road transport 18%, industrial
  processes 16.5% of UK primary PM2.5.
  [parliament.uk](https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/163116/html/)
- **AQEG — The Potential Air Quality Impacts from Biomass Combustion**
  (2017) — Ecodesign stove ≈ up to 6× a Euro-6 HGV's PM2.5.
  [uk-air.defra.gov.uk](https://uk-air.defra.gov.uk/library/reports.php?report_id=946)
- **BEIS/Kantar — Burning in UK Homes and Gardens** (2020) — only 4% of
  indoor burners rely solely on solid fuel; most burners urban and
  affluent.
  [naei.energysecurity.gov.uk](https://naei.energysecurity.gov.uk/reports/burning-uk-homes-and-gardens-plus-number-annexes)
- **WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines** (2021) — PM2.5: 5 µg/m³ annual;
  no evidence of a safe threshold.
  [who.int](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240034228)
- **WHO — Household air pollution and health** (fact sheet) —
  ~3.2 million deaths/yr attributable.
  [who.int](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/household-air-pollution-and-health)
- **IARC Monographs Vol. 95** (2010) — household biomass smoke: Group 2A
  probably carcinogenic.
  [publications.iarc.fr](https://publications.iarc.fr/113)
- **Health Canada — Health effects of residential wood smoke** (2023) —
  wood smoke ~21% of Canadian PM2.5 vs ~12% all transport.
  [publications.gc.ca](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.917507/publication.html)
- **CARB — Final Short-Lived Climate Pollutant Reduction Strategy**
  (2020) — residential wood burning projected largest anthropogenic BC
  source in California by 2030.
  [arb.ca.gov](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/final_SLCP_strategy.pdf)
- **US EPA — Burn Wise** — certified stoves ~70% lower PM than old
  conventional stoves.
  [epa.gov](https://www.epa.gov/burnwise)
- **EMEP/EEA Air Pollutant Emission Inventory Guidebook** (2019) —
  1A4 small combustion emission factors (basis of the CMO table).
