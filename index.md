# Counterpoints

Evidence-backed rebuttals for contested topics. Every claim cited;
steelman first, rebuttal second; plain language up front, technical detail below.

## Topics

### [Residential wood burning & wood smoke](topics/woodsmoke/)

Wood smoke is the largest or one of the largest sources of fine particulate
pollution in the UK, EU and North America — and one of the most poorly
understood by the public. Eight common claims examined against the
peer-reviewed literature, plus a master evidence review, full bibliography,
figures, and slides.
